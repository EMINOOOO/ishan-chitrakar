Our Story

More Than a Meal — A Cultural Journey

Nestled in the heart of Kathmandu, R.V Cafe & Sekuwa Corner was born from a passion for authentic Nepali flavors and the warmth of shared moments. We believe every bite tells a story — of mountains, traditions, and the people who make Nepal extraordinary.

From our charcoal-grilled sekuwa prepared with generations-old recipes to our hand-crafted specialty coffees, every dish is a celebration of quality, authenticity, and love for food.

Sekuwa Culture

The Art of Grilling
